\subsection{Discussion}
\label{discussion}

\subsubsection{Interpreting the contrasts}

\textbf{TF-IDF advantage.} One plausible explanation is that repeated EMSCAD templates and strong
lexical cues suit a high-dimensional sparse model, while right truncation can remove late fields.
These are mechanisms for further controlled tests, not causes demonstrated by the present scores.
Full fine-tuning still falls below structured LR on four of five paired partitions.

\textbf{Adaptation value.} Full tuning improves on top-two tuning on all five splits for text and
four for fusion, by 0.106 and 0.037 mean test AP, although a second optimization seed lowers the text
gain to 0.075 on three of five splits. The extra trainable parameters are therefore worth their
roughly 35 additional seconds per partition here, but even full tuning stays below structured
LR on four of five partitions, so it is not the best value for this dataset.

\textbf{Fusion trade-off.} Structured fields add information that need not be present in truncated
text, and with top-two tuning they raise mean test AP by 0.050. Once the whole encoder is tuned, the
fused model loses 0.018 AP on average, so the text representation already captures what the fields
add. The classical ablation shows the same pattern at a smaller scale: a 0.019 AP gain for structured
LR on three of five splits.

\textbf{Dependence and thresholds.} Group-disjoint partitions stop a related-ad group from crossing a
boundary, but all rows of a group still contribute together to one test metric. The largest fraud
group supplies 10.7\%--32.2\% of a test partition's fraud ads, and the five largest supply
36.5\%--54.7\%, so the nominal 137--187 positives are not independent examples. Validation/test
reversals and wide F1 deviations also show that a threshold chosen by validation F1 does not
guarantee a stable operating point.

\textbf{One-class objective.} One-class SVM learns a boundary around known-real training examples,
not a fraud ranking objective. Fraudulent ads can resemble ordinary vacancies or form dense
templates, while unusual real ads need not be fraudulent. AP near prevalence and low precision are
therefore compatible with the method's objective and do not by themselves diagnose leakage.

\subsubsection{Validity and limitations}

\textbf{Grouping guarantee.} The exact rule blocks shared normalized descriptions, company profiles,
emails and phone identities across partitions. It does not guarantee separation of fuzzy duplicates,
paraphrased campaigns, employers, URLs, data sources or collection periods. No temporal, source-held
or external-dataset evaluation is available, so the reported estimand is limited to these saved
same-dataset grouped partitions.

\textbf{Selection history.} Full cleaned-data EDA informed feature design (the training-only
check in Section~\ref{data} confirms its clear decisions but not three borderline ones), and seed-0 test scores,
curves and explanations were inspected during development. Model fitting and threshold selection do
not read test labels, but this later inspection can influence reported choices. The results are thus
exploratory rather than a pristine confirmatory evaluation.

\textbf{Replication and inference.} The five partitions reuse and overlap the same 17,597 ads, and
rows within an exact related-ad group remain dependent. Means, sample standard deviations and win
counts are descriptive across selected resamplings, not five independent replicates. No statistical
significance, statistical equivalence or population-level superiority is claimed.

\textbf{Optimization confounding.} Transformer runs use one declared optimization seed per partition,
so the main tables mix split and seed sensitivity. The second-seed check for the full text model
(Section~\ref{results}) shows that optimization alone moves test AP by 0.047 on average on the same
split, about half the split-to-split spread; per-split wins for the adaptation contrast should
therefore be read with that margin in mind. Classical tuning instead uses compact grids, so the
search procedures are not matched across model families.

\textbf{Input and operating limits.} Fine-tuning truncates at 512 tokens. Measured class-dependent
field loss makes this a material limitation, but the audit does not quantify the predictive value of
removed tokens. Scores are not calibrated probabilities, thresholds are validated on a small and
partition-dependent sample, and no review capacity, false-alarm cost, abstention or human-moderation
study establishes deployment readiness.

\textbf{Data and runtime limits.} EMSCAD categories, placeholders and lexical patterns may reflect
one platform and historical collection process, so transfer to another board, language, fraud rate
or time period is unknown. Saved timings were not collected under a controlled common hardware
protocol and cannot support an end-to-end efficiency or deployment-cost ranking.
